Plan your first build

Work through these checks before ordering parts.

Set a goal and budget

Pick one priority: more grip, a different look or a different exhaust note. Include labour and alignment in your budget where applicable.

Verify fitment

Use your make, model, year, engine and trim to check the manufacturer’s fitment list. Ask your installer to confirm clearances before ordering.

Check road-use requirements

Check local requirements for the parts you plan to install and confirm any impact on your warranty or insurance.
